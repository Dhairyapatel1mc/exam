# 🧠 Statistical Concepts Used

## 1. Probability

Probability measures the likelihood of an event occurring.

Formula:

P(E) = Favorable Outcomes / Total Outcomes

Example:

P(Pass Exam)

---

## 2. Empirical Probability

Calculated using observed data.

Formula:

P(E) = Number of Times Event Occurred / Total Observations

---

## 3. Theoretical Probability

Calculated using mathematical reasoning.

Example:

P(Weekend) = 2 / 7

---

## 4. Random Variable

Let:

X = Number of students passing among 3 selected students.

This follows a Binomial Distribution.

---

## 5. Binomial Distribution

Formula:

P(X=x) = nCx × p^x × (1-p)^(n-x)

Used for calculating probabilities of success across repeated trials.

---

## 6. Mean of Binomial Distribution

Formula:

μ = np

---

## 7. Variance of Binomial Distribution

Formula:

σ² = np(1-p)

---

## 8. Joint Probability

Probability of two events occurring simultaneously.

Formula:

P(A ∩ B)

Example:

P(Group Discussion AND Pass)

---

## 9. Marginal Probability

Probability of a single event.

Formula:

P(A)

Example:

P(Pass)

---

## 10. Conditional Probability

Probability of an event given another event.

Formula:

P(A|B)

Example:

P(Pass | Group Discussion)

---

## 11. Independent Events

Events are independent if:

P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B)

---

## 12. Bayes Theorem

Formula:

P(A|B) = [P(B|A) × P(A)] / P(B)

Used to predict the probability of passing given high attendance.
